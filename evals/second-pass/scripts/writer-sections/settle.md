## SKILL = settle. Write 30 tasks.
The agent is mid-task in Sourdaw or the skills repo and hits a question that would stop work: an
ambiguous instruction, conflicting evidence (docs vs code, two patterns), an unmade technical choice,
or a request whose premise may be false ("the X", "fix", "again", "only"). José's message gives the
task and the sticking point, or contains the false premise. Hard = the answer is decidable from the
repo (code, tests, AGENTS.md, ADRs, history) but a fast agent either asks José needlessly, guesses
wrong, or works on a false premise; in a few tasks the choice really is José's (spend, legal, what the
product claims to be) and the right move is one sharp question with a recommendation. Key: the right
decision with evidence, or the right single question.
