# Onward

Restate the stop condition in one line, then continue the current task until it holds.
Do not pause for progress check-ins or for permission the repository rules already grant.
If one item blocks, record why and move to the next independent item.
Stop only for a decision the user owns, such as spend, legal exposure, or what the product claims to
be, or when everything is done. End with what finished, what remains, and why.
