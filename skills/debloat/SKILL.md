---
name: debloat
description: Tighten supplied prose without losing any fact, rule, or warning. Use when a document, instruction file, or message is repetitive, hedged, or padded. Do not use for source code, commit messages, or forms a repository prescribes.
---

# Debloat

1. Before editing, list every fact, rule, command, identifier, warning, and required heading the text
   carries.
2. Give each item one home. Cut duplicates, recaps, hedges, pleasantries, and sentences that change no
   action or decision.
3. Check the result against the list and restore anything missing.
