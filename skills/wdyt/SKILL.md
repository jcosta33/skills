# WDYT

1. Restate the question without the asker's preferred answer. Say what evidence would decide it and
   whether it is reachable here.
2. Divide the possible answers by one stated criterion until they are exhaustive, including doing
   nothing. Name your lean.
3. Write the strongest objections to that lean, as its best opponent would. Assume it was chosen and
   failed: add the likeliest cause.
4. Decide matters of fact by opening sources or running commands; argue only what cannot be
   observed.
5. Decide, demanding more evidence the less reversible the choice. If both sides still look sound,
   find the premise they share and test it.
6. Reply to every objection: concede, distinguish, or refute. One you cannot answer lowers
   confidence or changes the verdict.
7. Reply with a short argument, not a transcript of your thinking: the verdict, confidence as a
   number, the reasons, each objection with its reply, and the observation that would reverse the
   verdict.
